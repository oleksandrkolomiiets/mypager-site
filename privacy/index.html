MYPAGER

Privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

MyPager is a personal iPhone app that shows your calendar, tasks and weather on a small Bluetooth display that you own (the "pager"). This policy explains what the app reads, where that data goes, and how to remove it.

What MyPager reads

  • Google account identity. Your Google account's email address and account ID (the OpenID email and openid scopes). MyPager uses them to tell your signed-in accounts apart.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly). Events on the calendars you choose: titles, times, attendance status, and whether an event is new or cancelled.
  • Google Tasks, read-only (tasks.readonly). Tasks on the lists you choose: titles, due dates and completion state.
  • Weather location. The home coordinates you pick in Settings. MyPager does not use your device's location.

MyPager never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where your data goes

  • To your pager, over Bluetooth. The app turns your events, tasks and weather into short screen summaries and sends them to your own pager device over a direct Bluetooth Low Energy connection. The pager stores only the most recent screens.
  • Nowhere else under our control. MyPager has no server, no user accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.
  • Google. MyPager requests your data from Google's APIs using sign-in tokens issued to the app.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). MyPager sends your chosen home coordinates to get a weather forecast. Nothing else is sent.
  • Anthropic (optional). If you enter your own Claude API key, MyPager sends the weather forecast (temperatures, rain, wind and times) to Anthropic to write the one-line weather message. No calendar, task, account or location data is ever included. Without a key, nothing is sent and a built-in template is used.

Storage and retention

  • Sign-in tokens and the optional API key are stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone.
  • Synced data, settings, a diagnostics log and recent quick notes are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app removes its private storage. Signing out of an account in Settings deletes that account's tokens.
  • The pager keeps the last screens it was sent until new ones replace them.

Your controls

  • In Settings you choose which calendars and task lists sync, and you can remove any account.
  • You can revoke MyPager's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyPager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show your own calendar and tasks on your own pager. It is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by people.

Children

MyPager is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

MYPAGER

A pager for your desk.

MyPager is a small always-on screen that shows your next meetings, today's tasks and the weather. The MyPager iPhone app reads your Google Calendar and Google Tasks and keeps the screen up to date over Bluetooth.

MyPager is a personal project. The device is a Waveshare ESP32-S3 board with a 4.2-inch reflective LCD, and the app is distributed through TestFlight, not the App Store.

The app has read-only access to the Google accounts you connect, and that data stays between Google, your iPhone and your pager. The privacy policy has the details.
